\section{Risk model and calibration}

AUROC is undefined when a split has no failures (Allen--Cahn test) or when every
step fails (advection--diffusion \texttt{ood\_ic}); those cells are shown
as \texttt{--}. Coverage is empirical coverage of the split-conformal interval
at nominal $0.9$; the guarantee requires exchangeability, which the shifted
splits violate.
